# Commercial white-label permission

The same complete core is available under the free [Attribution License](LICENSE).
A separate, executed written agreement may waive its interface attribution
requirement for a named licensee and named product. This page does not grant
that waiver, quote a price, or constitute an executed contract.

## Proposed order terms

An actual agreement must identify the legal parties, authorized signatories,
covered product(s), affiliates/deployments if any, covered versions, fee and
effective date. The intended default is perpetual white-label permission for
the covered versions; update access and support have separate durations.
Ending support or update access does not by itself revoke valid permission
for versions already covered. Termination for breach follows the executed
agreement.

A grant must expressly state:
- Permission to remove or replace "Powered by Tegg Markdown" in the covered product.
- Whether contractor work, embedded redistribution, SaaS and on-premise delivery
  are covered, and which downstream recipients can use the shipped integration.
- Which version range or delivered artifacts are covered.
- Continuing copyright, license and third-party notice obligations.
- Separate scope, response expectations and acceptance criteria for paid services.

All contributed code must be covered by rights sufficient for the promised
grant. Third-party software retains its own license; no agreement with Tegg
can waive an unrelated holder's rights.

## Requesting permission

Start a licensing inquiry at https://github.com/tegg-studio/tegg-markdown/issues
using non-confidential information only. A maintainer will arrange an appropriate
private channel before receiving company details or contract material.
No account, license server or online activation is needed for free use.
The SDK does not disable editing, saving or undo when support expires.

This repository supplies the public license and a commercial policy.
Commercial agreements require their own completed terms and legal review.
